What you're setting up

A Claude subscription
A paid Claude account. The free plan can't run the tools this uses.
The Claude app
Claude on your computer. Its Code tab is where Claude works with your files.
Claude in Chrome
An add-on that lets Claude open job sites and fill in forms in your browser.
The Job Search Kit
A folder you download. It holds your CVs, your answers and the log of jobs applied to.
1

Subscribe to Claude

About 5 minutes

  1. Go to claude.ai and click Sign up. Signing in with Google is fine.
  2. Open the menu at the bottom-left (your name) → Settings → Billing, or go straight to claude.ai/upgrade.
  3. Choose Pro. It's the cheapest paid plan and enough to start. See claude.com/pricing for the price in your currency.
  4. Type your card details yourself. Never paste card details into a chat with Claude.
Pro or Max?Start with Pro. If Claude often says you've reached your usage limit for the day, you can move up to Max later.
2

Install the Claude app

About 5 minutes

  1. Go to claude.ai/download and download the app for your computer.
  2. Open the downloaded file and drag Claude into Applications. Then open it from Applications.
  3. Open the downloaded installer and follow the prompts. Then open Claude from the Start menu.
  4. Sign in with the same account as step 1.
  5. At the top of the app you'll see tabs such as Chat and Code. You'll use Code.
Don't see a Code tab?Update the app and check you're signed in to the account with the paid plan.
3

Set up Chrome

About 10 minutes

Claude fills in applications inside Google Chrome, so it needs Chrome plus the Claude add-on.

  1. No Chrome yet? Download it from google.com/chrome.
  2. In Chrome, open the Chrome Web Store, search for Claude and click Add to Chrome. Check the publisher says Anthropic.
  3. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome and pin Claude so it's always visible.
  4. Click the Claude icon and sign in with the same Claude account.
  5. Sign in to your job sites in this Chrome: LinkedIn, JobStreet, Indeed, whatever you use. Claude uses whichever account is signed in.
Sharing the computer?If someone else's LinkedIn or email is signed in on this Chrome, make your own Chrome profile (Chrome menu → Profiles → Add) so Claude never applies from the wrong account.
4

Download the kit

About 2 minutes

You don't need a GitHub account. It's one download.

Download the kit (ZIP)

  1. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the ZIP to unzip it.
  2. Open your Downloads folder, right-click the ZIP → Extract All… → Extract.
  3. You'll get a folder called jobsearch-kit-main. Rename it to jobsearch and move it to your Desktop, so it's easy to find. Don't leave it in Downloads.
Already use GitHub?

Open the repository, click Use this template → Create a new repository, set it to Private, and clone your copy. Your personal files are ignored by git, so they won't be uploaded.

5

Add your CVs

About 2 minutes

  1. Open the jobsearch folder, then the cv folder inside it.
  2. Copy your CV PDFs into it. If you have different CVs for different kinds of job, add them all.
  3. Give them clear names, like Sam_Lee_Engineering_CV.pdf.
Your files stay with you.Nothing in this folder is uploaded anywhere, except a CV Claude attaches to a job you asked it to apply for.
6

Set yourself up

About 15 minutes of answering questions

  1. Open the Claude app → Code.
  2. Start a new session and choose your jobsearch folder as the folder to work in. If it asks whether you trust the folder, say yes. It's your own folder.
  3. In the message box, type:

You typeset me up

Claude will then:

  • Check for Python, a small free program the kit needs. If it's missing:
    • A pop-up may offer to install “command line developer tools”. Click Install and wait for it to finish.
    • Install it from python.org/downloads. On the first installer screen, tick Add python.exe to PATH before clicking Install. Then close and reopen the Claude app.
  • Read your CVs and show you what it found. Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Ask you questions a few at a time: the jobs and cities you want, your salary, what you won't do, and the usual form questions such as notice period, driving licence and work rights.
Not sure about an answer?Say “ask me each time”. Claude will never make one up.

At the end, Claude saves everything in a file called HANDOFF.md. Open it and read it once. Every answer Claude puts on a form comes from this file or your CV.

7

Apply for jobs

Start small: 5 jobs the first time

  1. Make sure Chrome is open and the Claude add-on is signed in.
  2. In the Code tab, in your jobsearch folder, type something like:

You typeapply for jobs: 5 from LinkedIn, posted in the last 3 days

Claude then:

  1. searches the job site in your Chrome
  2. reads each ad and skips the ones that break your rules, noting why
  3. shows you a shortlist and waits for your OK
  4. applies one job at a time with the right CV and your saved answers
  5. writes every application, skip and failure into the log

You can watch it work in Chrome and type in the chat at any time: “skip this one”, “stop”, “use my business CV for this”.

What you do yourself

Claude hands these to you. That's on purpose, to keep you in control of anything personal or permanent.

When this comes upWhat you do
A password or sign-inType it yourself in Chrome, then tell Claude “done”.
Creating an account on a company careers siteClaude fills in the form, and you type the password.
“I agree to the terms” boxesClaude asks first. Read it, then tick it or tell Claude it's OK.
A question Claude has no saved answer forAnswer it in the chat. Claude saves it so it won't ask again.
IC, passport or bank numbersAlways you. Claude won't type them.
Messages to recruitersClaude shows you the draft and sends only after you say yes.
Online tests and video interviewsYou take them. Claude reminds you of deadlines it sees.
The app asks “Allow Claude to…?”The app is checking before Claude runs something. Click Allow if it matches what you asked for.

Every time after

Open the Claude app → Code → your jobsearch folder, and say what you want:

apply for 10 jobs from JobStreet and LinkedIn, last 3 days

apply to this job: paste the link

show me what you applied to this week

add remote jobs to my search

A fresh search every 2–3 days works better than one huge batch.

Two people on one computer?Say “set up a second person called sam”. Their CVs, answers and log stay completely separate. When it's their turn, say “apply for jobs for sam”.

If something goes wrong

Claude says Chrome isn't connected

Check that Chrome is open and the Claude add-on is installed and signed in with the same account as the app. Then ask Claude to try again.

“python3: command not found” or “python is not recognized”

Python isn't installed yet. See step 6. On Windows, reinstall it with Add python.exe to PATH ticked, then close and reopen the Claude app.

Claude used the wrong LinkedIn account

Sign the other account out of Chrome, or use your own Chrome profile (step 3). Tell Claude, so it adds a warning to your HANDOFF.md.

“You've reached your usage limit”

Pro has a usage limit that resets after a few hours. Wait, or move up to Max if it happens a lot.

A job site asks “are you a robot?”

Solve it yourself, then tell Claude to carry on. Claude won't solve these.

LinkedIn warns about unusual activity

Slow down. Use smaller batches spread across the day. Job sites don't like accounts that apply very fast.

I want to start my answers over

Delete HANDOFF.md and say “set me up” again. Your log of past applications stays, so nothing gets applied to twice.

Good habits

  • Check your first few applications on the job site's “Applied jobs” page.
  • Keep HANDOFF.md honest and up to date. It speaks for you on every form.
  • Ten good-fit applications beat fifty random ones.
  • Copy the folder to a USB drive or cloud storage now and then. The log is your record of where you've applied.
